Offense

Tap shoot without turbo to pump fake. Hold to jump, then release in the green zone. Hold near the rim to dunk; landing with an unreleased shot is travel. Take off outside the arc for a three-point dunk.

Hold pass to set up a lob, then release while your teammate is airborne. A CPU finishes automatically; a human receiver presses shoot. Off ball, hold call to request it.

Defense

Hold jockey to guard. Shoot becomes steal against a dribbler and block against a shot or dunk. Turbo + jockey shoves on defense: it costs stamina and can stun on contact.

Alternate the two escape keycaps to break ice or wake up. Holding one key does not count.

Fire & Ultimates

Three consecutive makes ignite 15 seconds of fire. Each make adds five seconds. An opposing basket or a direct steal ends it; missing does not. Non-scoring attacks trigger on cooldown on either side of the ball.

Baskets, steals and blocks charge your ultimate. A typed aura and READY portrait mean it is available. Activate while holding the ball; turbo forces a dunk.

Match

Four two-minute quarters, a 24-second shot clock and overtime for ties. Solo control follows your team's ball holder; switch off ball to defend with your other mon. Co-op keeps both players assigned.

Arrows also move Player 1 in solo. Pause, lineup and settings are available in the toolbar.
