# Crossy Road Clone (CROSSYMON)

This is a remake of Crossy Road but with Pikachu as a sprite (I wanted to add pokeball powerups but ran out of credits *sigh*)

## How to play
WASD/Arrow keys to move; +1 in score when you move forward (to a place in vertical space you've never been)

## AI models
I used GPT 5.6 and quick spec on Kiro. I also had it consistently ask me questions as to what I wanted it to do
